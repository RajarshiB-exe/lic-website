About LIC

Life Insurance Corporation of India (LIC) is the largest and most trusted life insurance company in India. Established in 1956, LIC has been a cornerstone of financial security for millions of individuals and families across the country.

LIC provides a wide range of policies including protection plans, savings plans, pension solutions, and health coverage, helping customers achieve long-term financial stability and security.

About Serampore Branch 444

The Serampore Branch 444 serves customers across the Hooghly district with reliable, customer-focused insurance services. The branch is known for its efficient policy servicing, claim assistance, and professional advisory support.

With experienced advisors and a strong commitment to customer satisfaction, the branch ensures that clients receive the best financial guidance tailored to their needs.
